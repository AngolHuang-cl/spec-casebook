## pm-rules — PM global rules and per-class rules

- **Global Rule** (ref. 「AIGC Watermark Spec.pptx」, SharePoint, not readable here):
  - Image: AIGC watermark stamped at the top-left. 圖檔 link → PDR-Main-Page 20538:218619 (same AI label and 12% note).
  - Video: AIGC watermark at the top-left for the **first two seconds**.
  - Audio: a canned clip 「This audio was generated by AI.」 added at the start, **localized** (需要多國). The clip text
    links to a SharePoint audio file (not readable here).
- **Scope:** Video Edit by Chat, Image to Video, Cinematic Reel Creator, Character Motion Swap, Video Object Removal,
  AI Storytelling, Text to Video, AI Art, Text to Image, AI Anime Video, AI Anime, AI Cartoon, AI Sketch, Voice Changer,
  AI Text to Speech, Talking Avatar, 「Music Generator → Server」 (its watermark is added by the server).
- **My Artwork page:**
  - Video: thumbnail no watermark; View no watermark; Full Editor: no watermark on the timeline;
    Save, Share: watermark by the Preference setting (first two seconds only).
  - Image: thumbnail no; View no; Full Editor timeline no. (No Save / Share line: Q-4.)
  - Pure audio: Preview hears 「This audio was generated by AI」; Download hears it; **(red, newest)** Add to timeline:
    the clip is not heard; **(red)** Export, Share: by the Preference setting, the project's 0–2 s gets the icon
    watermark or not.
- **Timeline with Text-2-Speech, Voice Changer, AI Anime, AI Cartoon, AI Sketch AI content:** Timeline shows the
  watermark or not by the Preference setting; Export, Share: the icon watermark by the Preference setting.
- **Note:** AI content already downloaded is not updated when the Preference setting changes later.
- **Example (Group 3):** two clips 「非 AI 素材」 (non-AI) then 「AI 素材」 (AI); the AI badge sits on the top-left of
  the **first, non-AI** clip: the icon goes on the project's first 2 s wherever the AI clip is (Q-9).
- **My Artwork flow (images 8–10):** AI Magic › My Artwork › Image to Video tile → 「Image to Video」 task list
  (「Tasks are stored on the cloud for 1 year…」, 「View」) → result page (Share Video: YouTube, TikTok, IG Reels,
  Instagram, FB Reels; 「Full Editor」, 「Save」; 「You might also like」).

## Jira PDRIOS-722 (pasted 2026-11-17)

- Scope: result video, image, audio. Watermark visible in exported / generated results; consistent across export,
  share and save.
- AI label 12% of the image's short side, padding included. Audio watermark multi-language.
- 「meta data server side會always打sign」 — overridden by the sheet (REQ-002).
- 「UI setting 是控 visible watermark是否開or關」 — the switch controls only the visible watermark; metadata is not
  affected by it.
- Metadata method: SharePoint folder 「_2607_C2PA/kit」 (not readable here, Q-12).
